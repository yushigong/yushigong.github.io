Mobile QA report — October 10, 2026

Scope
- index.html (home)
- about.html
- dpod.html
- ccc.html
- ges.html
- fitto.html
- shopify.html
- tcr.html
- energy.html
- playground.html

Method
- Rendered local HTML and local stylesheets in headless Chromium.
- Checked viewport widths: 320, 360, 375, 390, 414, 430, 480, 576, 640, 720, 768, 820, 860, 900, 1000, 1024, and 1280 pixels.
- Inspected document/body scroll widths and key navigation/chapter-heading geometry.
- Remote CDN styles and page JavaScript were not fully exercised; a Bootstrap 3 layout approximation was used for static testing where needed.

Results
- 10 pages x 17 viewport widths = 170 layout checks.
- 0 page-level horizontal-overflow cases in the tested render after the fixes.
- Long chapter labels in case studies can wrap instead of running outside their text area.
- Related-project cards stack and align to the content area on mobile.
- Decorative homepage marquee overflow is clipped at narrower tablet widths.
- Mobile nav items did not overlap in the checked widths.

Limitations
- This does not replace final live-device testing with real image/video assets, external font/CDN loading, JavaScript-driven animations, touch behavior, and browser-specific rendering.
- Recommended final spot-check: iPhone Safari at 375/390/430px, a compact 320px viewport, and Android Chrome.
